
- **Enrollment** — walks the user through enrolling the device in Intune (device admin, work profile, or fully managed depending on the enrollment method configured)
- **App access** — lets users browse and install company-approved apps assigned to them (the "company app store")
- **Compliance status** — shows whether the device is compliant/non-compliant, and what's failing (e.g., missing PIN, encryption off) — with self-remediation steps
- **Device info & actions** — lets the user check device details, sync manually, or in some cases retire/remove company data from their own device
- **Sign-in gate** — often required before the user can access other work apps (Outlook, Teams) if enrollment/compliance is enforced

> Company Portal also handles **Android Enterprise enrollment flows** (work profile setup, fully managed device provisioning)
