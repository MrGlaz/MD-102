
* **Retire** removes a device from Intune management without erasing personal data. (wont be enrolled again)
* **Wipe** erases all company data and applications + erase personal data. (will be used again)
* **Reset** performs a complete factory reset as if the device is brand new.

> Device Cleanup rules (wipe for inactivity between 30 and 270 days)

To remove a stale devices >> delete the devices
> Device Cleanup rule does not delete a device from a subscription
