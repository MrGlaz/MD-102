App protection policies = define enforcing requirements inside apps (on managed/unmanaged device)
Conditional Access = enforces the rules at sign-in

iOS/Android = data protection, access requirements, and conditional launch

App can be installed but if not meeting compliance requirements = cannot access data

[[App Protection Policies]] > Key compliance controls
- **Access requirements**: require a PIN, biometric, or corporate credentials.
- **Conditional launch**: block access or wipe company data if the device is jailbroken or rooted, if the OS is out of date, or if device health checks fail.
- **Data protection**: restrict copy and paste, prevent save-as to personal locations, and limit offline access.
